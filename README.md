## What is Madhoganj?

Madhoganj is a free, single-file web dashboard built for Indian farmers — starting with Bazpur, Udham Singh Nagar, Uttarakhand. No app download. No login. No subscription. Works on any phone browser.

The name comes from a farming region in UP. The project is personal — the founder's father farms after retirement. This is the agriculture vertical of [AAA — Artigellence Augmentation Aggregator](https://aaa.artigellence.com).

## The Gap We Fill

Plantix had 34M users and a mission to cut pesticide use. VC money arrived and it became a pesticide e-commerce platform. **Madhoganj has no VC.** The recommendation engine is not owned by a pesticide company. The mission stays.

Three things Madhoganj does that others don't — simultaneously:

1. **Why** the disease happened — root cause, not just identification
2. **Organic alternatives ranked first** — never paid placements
3. **Regional crop-diversity intelligence** at village level (5 / 10 / 25 km radius)

## The Arthiya Problem

Commission agents (arthiyas/arathiyas) are licensed intermediaries who buy from farmers at mandi. They also double as moneylenders at 12–24% interest. Farmers often don't know today's mandi price before they sell.

Madhoganj shows:
- Today's modal price at 3 nearest mandis
- 7-day price trend
- MSP / SAP floor in bold — so no inspector can bluff below it
- Best mandi comparison

**Arthiya bypass — not by eliminating him, but by informing the farmer before the sale.**
